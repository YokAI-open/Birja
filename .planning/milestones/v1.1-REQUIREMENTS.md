# Requirements: Birja

**Milestone:** v1.1 — US Stock Trading
**Defined:** 2026-11-10
**Status:** Requirements and roadmap approved (2026-11-10)
**Core value:** Make a trade at a real, recent market price and see what it is worth now, correct to the cent.

## v1.1 Requirements

Owner approved all 26 requirements and the exact boundaries below, including a strict source age below 120 seconds, midpoint stock valuation, gross ordinary USD dividends without tax/reinvestment, and safe handling of post-payment corrections. No implementation or external account action is authorized by requirements approval.

### Catalogue and permitted market data

- [ ] **STOCK-01**: User can browse exactly 20 curated NYSE/Nasdaq USD common stocks alongside crypto, with stable identities, company names, symbols and listing exchanges.
- [ ] **STOCK-02**: User sees “IEX prices” on stock listings, trade previews and stock valuations, with an explanation of single-venue coverage and simulated execution.
- [ ] **STOCK-03**: User sees stock bid/ask prices and their source-event timestamp, refreshed about every 60 seconds; stock execution refuses source quotes aged 120 seconds or more, invalid quotes and previous-session quotes at reopening.
- [ ] **DATA-01**: Users receive live stock quotes and corporate-action information only after the selected products’ access, display/derived-data rights, quotas and required attribution are verified for Birja; otherwise stock activation remains disabled without affecting crypto.

### Market availability

- [ ] **MKT-01**: User can execute stocks only during the applicable regular US session, with holidays, early closes and America/New_York daylight-saving changes applied server-side.
- [x] **MKT-02**: User sees a clear refusal for closed, halted, frozen or unknown/stale eligibility state; new fills recheck eligibility and quote age after blocking database waits, while crypto remains independently available.

### Stock trading and shared cash

- [x] **STKTR-01**: User can buy and sell positive whole-share quantities only; fractional-stock and dollar-input stock orders are rejected, while existing fractional and dollar-based crypto orders retain their behavior.
- [x] **STKTR-02**: User receives a simulated stock buy at an eligible IEX ask and sell at an eligible IEX bid, with zero commission and zero additional spread; omitted regulatory fees and simulated execution are disclosed.
- [x] **STKTR-03**: User sees an itemized server preview before confirmation and the actual persisted fill afterward; a relevant executable-price change returns a revised preview requiring renewed confirmation.
- [x] **STKTR-04**: User spends and receives the same existing virtual USD balance across stocks and crypto, without another starting grant; concurrent cross-asset orders cannot overspend cash or holdings.
- [x] **STKTR-05**: User can retry an uncertain stock order without duplicate execution; replay returns the stored original fill even after the market closes, and a changed payload conflicts.
- [x] **STKTR-06**: User can immediately reuse stock-sale proceeds for stocks or crypto, with the absence of real settlement restrictions disclosed.

### Combined portfolio and event history

- [x] **PORT-06**: User sees combined cash, crypto and stock holdings, allocation and portfolio return against the original stored starting cash; eligible stock valuation uses IEX bid/ask midpoint and dividend cash is counted once.
- [x] **PORT-07**: User can inspect dated stock marks while the market is closed; missing or corporate-action-uncertain valuations are explicitly unavailable and make aggregate totals incomplete rather than silently zero or fresh.
- [x] **HIST-03**: User sees stock fills, Dividend credits, forward-split events and explicit corrections in a private, deterministic newest-first paginated History, with event-specific details and preserved existing crypto fills.

### Dividends

- [x] **DIV-01**: User dividend entitlements use structured ordinary USD cash-dividend announcements whose identities, dates, amounts and revisions are retained and regularly rechecked; conflicting, incomplete, cancelled or uncertain events do not cause guessed payments.
- [x] **DIV-02**: User dividend entitlement is based on holdings at the verified eligibility cutoff for that event, accounting for historical trades and processed splits; buying after the cutoff does not earn the dividend and selling afterward does not erase it.
- [x] **DIV-03**: User receives the verified gross ordinary USD cash dividend into shared cash on or after its payable date, without tax withholding or automatic reinvestment; the Dividend event shows stock, relevant dates, eligible quantity, per-share rate and credited amount.
- [x] **DIV-04**: User cannot receive duplicate dividend cash from retries, concurrent processing, revised announcements or recovery; credit and History publication are one atomic accounting operation.
- [x] **DIV-05**: User can trace a verified post-payment correction through a separate adjustment linked to the original event; prior history is retained, duplicate deltas are prevented and uncertain or unaffordable negative adjustments are held for reconciliation instead of making cash negative.

### Splits and uncertain corporate actions

- [x] **ACT-01**: User holdings are automatically reconciled for verified straightforward forward splits when entitlements are unambiguous and preserve supported whole-share quantities; total holding basis is conserved, unit basis changes correctly and historical trade fills remain unchanged.
- [x] **ACT-02**: User cannot trade an affected stock while a reverse split, merger, fractional entitlement, disputed action or other unsupported event requires reconciliation; its uncertain valuation is unavailable until a verified, auditable resolution, without guessed liquidation or cash-in-lieu.
- [x] **ACT-03**: User cannot receive repeated split adjustments after retries or recovery; action processing is ordered with trades and dividend eligibility, and source-monitoring failures have explicit suspension/recovery rules.

### Compatibility and acceptance

- [ ] **SAFE-01**: Existing users retain their accounts, balances, instrument identities, holdings and immutable crypto history through reviewed migrations; mixed-asset services continue to work with restricted PostgreSQL roles and verified TLS.
- [x] **SAFE-02**: Operator can recover mixed stock/crypto holdings, dividend entitlements/payments, action revisions and event history from an exact encrypted backup with consistent totals and duplicate-prevention state; schema, grant and retained recovery-tool compatibility are proven before rollout.
- [x] **SAFE-03**: User stock journeys are verified through real isolated PostgreSQL accounting/concurrency tests and browser flows for open/closed sessions, dividends, splits, stale data and crypto coexistence; applicable CI coverage/security gates remain enforced.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---|---|
| Real brokerage orders, funds or deposits | Birja remains a virtual-money simulator |
| Margin, short selling and leverage | Outside the agreed whole-share cash-account scope |
| Consolidated SIP feed or 15-minute-delay fallback | Owner selected labelled IEX with crypto-like freshness |
| Unapproved paid subscriptions, provider contact or agreement acceptance | Feed selection does not authorize external commitments |
| Guessing missing corporate-action amounts or fractional cash-in-lieu | Suspend uncertain affected instruments for verified reconciliation |
| Rewriting or resetting existing accounts/fills | Existing balances and financial history must be preserved |
