## Phase Goal

**As a** friend trying YokaiExchange, **I want to** sign in with a code sent to my email and stay signed in, **so that** I get my $10,000.00 practice account exactly once and can come back to it without a password.

<objective>
Slice 2 of sign-in: a user who got a code can prove it, and the first successful proof creates their user and their $10,000.00 account, once. This adds the first domain tables (`users`, `accounts`) with a hand-reviewed migration, the verify-code endpoint, and the "exactly once" guarantees enforced by the schema. No session cookies yet (02-04 adds them on top of this same endpoint).

Purpose: ACCT-01/ACCT-02 are guaranteed by Postgres constraints and an `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, not by app checks, so races and retries can never grant cash twice.
Output: models, migration `0002`, `services/accounts.py`, `verify_code` service + route, tests.
</objective>
