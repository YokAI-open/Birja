## Phase Goal

**As a** signed-in Birja user, **I want to** buy and sell coins at real, recent market prices with the full cost shown before I confirm, **so that** I can practise trading and get a result that is correct to the cent.

<objective>
Add the trading schema: `holdings` (one row per user and coin, with cost basis) and `trades` (append-only fill records with the idempotency key), plus the whole-cents invariant on `accounts.cash` in a separate, guarded migration.

Purpose: the database is the last line of defence for money (CLAUDE.md); 04-06 relies on these constraints as backstops behind the row lock.
Output: models, migrations 0006 and 0007, migration test updates, constraint tests.
</objective>
