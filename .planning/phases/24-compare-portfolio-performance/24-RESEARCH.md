## Implementation patterns

### Capture the same financial projection

- `load_portfolio_snapshots` selects one owner's active roots, at most ten, with cash, stored starting cash and positive holdings in a coherent statement. It uses admitted featured and catalog stock valuation and rechecks current ownership and market authority. Reuse that pipeline for daily capture. Keep the existing app read and response shape. `PortfolioSnapshot` already names the in-memory current valuation, so give the new persisted row a distinct name such as `PortfolioDailyValue`. [Verified statement implementation](../../../backend/app/services/portfolio.py)
- `calculate_portfolio` sums Decimal quantity × midpoint plus cash, values each position separately, uses the actual stored starting cash and rounds at the response boundary with half-even cents. A missing mark or unsafe stock units makes complete total and return null. A zero starting cash has no percentage return. Preserve existing mark decisions for fresh, dated closed or halted, and missing inputs, including current crypto age and availability treatment. A cash-only root can have a valid recorded value. Keep a nullable complete total and the original starting cash in the persisted contract, with no repeated price arithmetic in the new service. [Verified calculator](../../../backend/app/services/portfolio_math.py) · [Verified featured and catalog marks](../../../backend/app/services/stock_valuation.py)
- Share the minimal valuation settings consumed by the scheduler and API. `Settings` has price freshness and stale thresholds, while `SchedulerSettings` owns a different secret surface and currently lacks those fields. Ensure overridden settings agree at the normal Compose entry points. Reuse the existing pure time seam for deterministic tests and obtain an advancing UTC time for normal capture and authority rechecks. [Verified configuration](../../../backend/app/core/config.py) · [Dev Compose](../../../docker-compose.yml) · [Prod Compose](../../../docker-compose.prod.yml)
- Suggested daily policy is one first committed observation per UTC day in a fixed evening capture window, e.g. 22:00 UTC through midnight. Take the actual value and timestamp when the job runs. On restart resume eligible work within that UTC day. After midnight leave a missed day absent. Do not reconstruct a prior day's prices or move yesterday's observation onto today's date. Label the UTC day consistently and display the actual observation time in the user's locale. Treat a valid but incomplete valuation as a committed null point for the day. Treat an interrupted transaction as eligible for retry. [Base scheduler cadence](../../../backend/app/tasks/scheduler.py) · [Capture-forward scope](../../REQUIREMENTS.md)

### Serialize capture against terminal closure

The statement loader ends with `FOR SHARE`, while restricted `birja_scheduler` has SELECT on accounts and the app has a column-level cash UPDATE grant. PostgreSQL requires UPDATE privilege on at least one column even for `FOR SHARE`. Follow the existing lock-only, migrator-owned SQL function pattern for a narrow scheduler fence that checks exact owner, portfolio ID and `closed_at IS NULL`. Keep the shared lock through the insert and commit. No account UPDATE grant, broader lock, stock instrument lock or network I/O is needed for this write. Preserve the existing portfolio response's final fence. If closure commits first, collect nothing. If capture commits first, closure retains that point and stops later captures. [Verified grants](../../../backend/app/db/portfolio_bootstrap.py) · [Verified lock pattern](../../../backend/alembic/versions/0017_portfolio_ownership.py) · [Verified terminal transition](../../../backend/alembic/versions/0023_portfolio_closure_authority.py) · [PostgreSQL locking privilege](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-select.html#SQL-FOR-UPDATE-SHARE)

Run a bounded background callback in the dedicated scheduler while capture is enabled. Enumerate active roots by ID in small batches and commit in short owner/portfolio transactions. A bad or newly closed root must allow the remainder of the batch and later roots to proceed. Keep a bounded scan position or equivalent fair selection so a failing root cannot starve later work. Let the database's unique `(portfolio_id, utc_day)` key and `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` handle concurrent ticks, retries and restarts. Redis, if used for scheduling convenience, has no standing as the evidence of capture. Test the two closure orders and duplicate jobs with separate real database sessions. [Verified scheduler lifecycle](../../../backend/app/tasks/scheduler.py) · [PostgreSQL conflict semantics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-insert.html#SQL-ON-CONFLICT) · [SQLAlchemy PostgreSQL INSERT](https://docs.sqlalchemy.org/en/20/dialects/postgresql.html#insert-on-conflict-upsert)
