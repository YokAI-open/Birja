# Birja 0.7.1 — Markets and trade ticket polish

- Markets keeps the last verified stock price visible during a confirmed
  closure and shows a countdown to the next session. Trading stays disabled
  while the market is closed.
- Trade tickets show the portfolio name in the switcher and keep order
  confirmation and recovery tied to that portfolio.
- The refreshed Birja logo and icon appear in the header and browser tab.

This patch uses the existing database schema and configuration. Live stock
activation remains a separate follow-up.
