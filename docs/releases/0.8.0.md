# Birja 0.8.0 — Searchable US stock trading

- Search the admitted NYSE and Nasdaq common-stock catalog by ticker or
  company name. Listings keep their identity through reviewed ticker changes.
- Eligible stocks support simulated whole-share buys at the IEX ask and
  sells at the bid, with zero stock commission. A price change requires
  another confirmation, and interrupted orders can recover their original
  receipt. Markets keeps the last permitted price and reopening countdown
  during a verified close.
- Stock trades and eligible dividends and splits flow into the selected
  portfolio and its private History. Crypto remains available independently.

This release includes PostgreSQL schema `0026` and the corresponding encrypted
recovery tooling. Production stock trading remains pending the approved
rollout, expanded catalog and data qualification, and a live IEX round trip
outside the original 20 stocks.
